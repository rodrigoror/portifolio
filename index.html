Migration · Data integration

Moving 100,000 card customers between processors without anyone noticing

Challenge
Migrate every credit-card customer from FIS to Pismo, with no room for downtime or refused transactions.
What I did
Led 10 people and coordinated ~20 more from Security, DevOps and five partners. Designed the migration app: field mapping, shadow-mode reconciliation and rollback at every stage.
Zero downtime. Zero refused transactions.

Architecture · Multi-tenant SaaS

A collections platform that became a B2B product

Challenge
Turn an internal collections product into a platform partners could use.
What I did
Architected the multi-tenant Wolfe platform: Node.js/TypeScript, GraphQL, MongoDB and AWS Cognito, processing 400,000+ records a day for four tenants, three of them external.
Revenue tripled. Partner onboarding went from weeks to days.
Why GraphQL made the difference →
AI-native engineering

Building AI into every stage of delivery

Challenge
Ship faster without trading away quality in a regulated fintech.
What I did
Agents challenge the spec, Claude Code and Cursor write the code, CodeRabbit and a business-rules RAG agent review it, and tests come from the spec. Built Claude skills and MCP servers (database, Grafana, Kubernetes) so agents work on real systems.
80% higher productivity (cycle time). 80% fewer production war-rooms.
The playbook →
AI in production · Cost

The AI chatbot that worked, and why we turned it off

Challenge
Reduce the support queue with a WhatsApp chatbot (Salesforce + Gemini on Vertex AI).
What I did
Took it from operator copilot to customer-facing, branch by branch. When the cloud bill grew 10x in three months, I shut it down and used AI to fix the flow itself.
Resolution 25% → 93%. Then 70% without a model in every conversation.
